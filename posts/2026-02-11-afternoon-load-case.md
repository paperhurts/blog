title: The afternoon slump is a load case
date: 2026-02-11 15:30
draft: true
---

I used to treat three o'clock as a character flaw. Now I treat it like a load case. You don't blame a beam for deflecting; you size it for the load it actually sees. So: hard thinking before lunch, easy wins after, and nothing important on the calendar at 3:15.
