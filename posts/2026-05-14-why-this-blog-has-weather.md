Most websites look the same at noon and at midnight. That always felt a little dishonest for something that's supposed to be a journal. People have weather. So does this. Sometimes it rains while you read, and that isn't a bug.

## How it works

Every post sits at the hour it was written. Scroll the front page and you're scrolling through a day: early posts under a sunrise, late ones under the stars. The weather does its own thing on top of that, the way weather does. The mechanics of the rain are in [[How to draw rain with a for-loop]].

Posts written after midnight stay in the night they belong to, instead of jumping to the top of the next morning. See [[one-more-commit]].

## Writing one

A post is a markdown file with a title and the time you wrote it:

```md
---
title: The ten minutes after a storm
date: 2026-09-09 19:24
---
```

That's the whole ritual.
